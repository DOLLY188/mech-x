Engineering Reference

These calculators are simplified engineering tools intended for learning, preliminary calculations and project development. Always verify important engineering calculations using accepted standards, textbooks, manufacturer data and appropriate safety factors.

Torque = Force × Radius
Power = Torque × Angular Velocity
Pressure = Force ÷ Area
Stress = Force ÷ Area
Efficiency = (Output Power ÷ Input Power) × 100

About MECH-X

MECH-X is an experimental engineering platform designed to bring engineering theory into an interactive digital environment.

Instead of only reading formulas, users can manipulate engineering parameters and observe how systems respond.

The long-term goal is to combine mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, robotics, control systems and automotive engineering into one accessible engineering laboratory.

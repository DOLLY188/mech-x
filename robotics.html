PID Controller

A PID controller calculates a control signal using three actions: proportional, integral and derivative control.

u(t) = Kp·e(t) + Ki∫e(t)dt + Kd·de(t)/dt

The controller continuously compares the desired setpoint with the actual system output.

What the Parameters Do

Kp
Determines how strongly the controller reacts to the current error.
Ki
Accumulates past error and helps remove steady-state error.
Kd
Reacts to the rate of change of error and can reduce overshoot.

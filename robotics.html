Forward Kinematics

Forward kinematics determines the position of a robot's end effector from the known joint angles and link lengths.

x = L₁ cos(θ₁) + L₂ cos(θ₁ + θ₂)

y = L₁ sin(θ₁) + L₂ sin(θ₁ + θ₂)

Where:

L₁ = length of the first link
L₂ = length of the second link
θ₁ = first joint angle
θ₂ = second joint angle

Engineering note: This simulator uses a simplified planar two-degree-of-freedom robotic arm model. It is intended for learning and visualization of forward kinematics.

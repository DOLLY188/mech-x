Engineering Model

Normal Stress: σ = F / A
Normal Strain: ε = ΔL / L
Young's Modulus: E = σ / ε
Factor of Safety: FOS = Yield Strength / Working Stress
Rectangular Beam: I = bh³ / 12
Point Load Deflection: δ = PL³ / 48EI
UDL Deflection: δ = 5wL⁴ / 384EI
Solid Shaft: J = πd⁴ / 32
Torsional Stress: τ = Tc / J
Angle of Twist: θ = TL / JG
Spring Rate: k = Gd⁴ / 8D³n
Mechanical Power: P = Tω
Engineering note: These tools use simplified engineering models intended for learning, preliminary calculations and simulation. Real engineering designs should account for material properties, geometry, boundary conditions, fatigue, safety factors, manufacturing tolerances and applicable engineering standards.

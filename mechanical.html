Engineering Models

ε = ΔL / L
E = σ / ε

Engineering note: These calculations use idealized engineering models. Real component design must consider material properties, loading conditions, dimensions, manufacturing tolerances, fatigue, stress concentrations, temperature, safety factors and applicable engineering standards.

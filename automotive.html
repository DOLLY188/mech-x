Vehicle Dynamics Model

The simulation estimates longitudinal vehicle motion using engine torque, transmission ratios, tyre radius, aerodynamic drag, rolling resistance and road gradient.

Wheel Torque = Engine Torque × Gear Ratio × Final Drive × Efficiency
Ftractive = Twheel / r
Fdrag = ½ρCdAv²
Frolling = Crrmg
a = (Ftractive − Fdrag − Frolling − Fgrade) / m

This is an educational vehicle-dynamics model. Real vehicles have additional effects including detailed engine maps, transmission losses, tyre slip, suspension behaviour and thermal limitations.

Engineering Model

The simulator combines several fundamental vehicle-dynamics equations.

Wheel Torque

Twheel = Tengine × G × FD × η

Engine torque is multiplied by the selected gear ratio and final-drive ratio before reaching the wheels.

Tractive Force

Ftractive = Twheel / r

Wheel torque is converted into longitudinal force at the tyre.

Aerodynamic Drag

Fdrag = ½ρCdAv²

Aerodynamic resistance increases approximately with the square of vehicle velocity.

Rolling Resistance

Frr = Crrmg

Rolling resistance represents losses caused by tyre deformation and road interaction.

Grade Resistance

Fgrade = mg sin(θ)

A positive road slope increases the force required to move the vehicle.

Vehicle Acceleration

a = (Ftractive − Fdrag − Frr − Fgrade) / m

The net longitudinal force determines the vehicle acceleration.

What the Simulator Considers

Engine Torque and power limits are considered when calculating wheel force.
Transmission Different gear ratios determine the mechanical advantage at the wheels.
Tyres The tyre-road friction coefficient limits the maximum usable traction force.
Aerodynamics Drag increases strongly as vehicle speed increases.
Road Rolling resistance and road slope affect the required driving force.
Numerical Simulation Vehicle speed is calculated step-by-step using a time-domain numerical model.

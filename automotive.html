Engineering Model

The simulator uses simplified vehicle-dynamics equations.

Wheel Torque

Twheel = Tengine × Gear Ratio × Final Drive × η

The transmission multiplies engine torque before it reaches the wheels.

Tractive Force

Ftractive = Twheel / r

Wheel torque is converted into a forward force at the tyre contact patch.

Aerodynamic Drag

Fdrag = ½ρCdAv²

Air resistance increases rapidly as vehicle speed rises.

Rolling Resistance

Frr = Crrmg

Rolling resistance represents energy lost through the tyres and road interaction.

Vehicle Acceleration

a = (Ftractive − Fdrag − Frr − Fslope) / m

The net force acting on the vehicle determines its acceleration.

Power-to-Weight Ratio

P/W = Power / Mass

Power-to-weight provides a useful first-level comparison of vehicle performance.

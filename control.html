Basic PID concepts and simple calculations

What is PID?

P (Proportional): Reacts to current error. Higher Kp → stronger correction.

I (Integral): Corrects accumulated past error. Helps remove steady-state error.

D (Derivative): Reacts to how fast the error is changing. Helps reduce overshoot.

Simplified Formula:
Error = Setpoint – Process Value
Output = (Kp × Error) + (Ki × Integral) + (Kd × Derivative)

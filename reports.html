Robotics Kinematics

Forward kinematics calculates the end-effector position from known joint angles.

x = L₁ cos(θ₁) + L₂ cos(θ₁ + θ₂)

y = L₁ sin(θ₁) + L₂ sin(θ₁ + θ₂)

Inverse kinematics performs the opposite task: given a desired X/Y position, it calculates the joint angles required to reach that position.

cos(θ₂) = (x² + y² - L₁² - L₂²) / (2L₁L₂)

θ₂ = acos(cos(θ₂))
Engineering note: Not every target position is reachable. The target must fall within the robot's reachable workspace. This simulator uses a simplified planar two-degree-of-freedom model.

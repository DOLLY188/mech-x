Engineering Model

The simulator uses a simplified DC motor model. Electrical and mechanical equations are solved numerically over small time steps.

Back EMF: E = Ke × ω
Armature Current: I = (V − E) / R
Motor Torque: T = Kt × I
J(dω/dt) = T − Tload − bω
Mechanical Power: P = T × ω

Engineering note: This is an educational simulation. The model assumes constant motor parameters and does not include detailed magnetic saturation, temperature effects, nonlinear friction, switching electronics, or detailed motor construction.

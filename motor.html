Engineering Model

The simulator uses a simplified mathematical model of a separately excited DC motor. The electrical and mechanical behavior is calculated numerically over small time steps.

Back EMF: E = Ke × ω
Armature Current: I = (V − E) / R
Electromagnetic Torque: T = Kt × I
J(dω/dt) = T − Tload − bω
Angular Velocity: ω = 2πN / 60
Electrical Input Power: Pin = V × I
Mechanical Output Power: Pout = T × ω
Efficiency: η = (Pout / Pin) × 100

Engineering note: This is an educational simulation. It assumes constant Ke, Kt, resistance, inertia and viscous friction. Real motors may exhibit magnetic saturation, temperature-dependent resistance, nonlinear friction, brush losses, switching effects and other nonlinear behavior.
